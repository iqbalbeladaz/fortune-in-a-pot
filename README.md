## The entry

> This week the cat gives up your windowsill, the sun saves its warmest spot for you, and one brave new leaf appears.

One line, 115 characters (gate: 1–120).

## Why this fortune should win

- **Unmistakably a houseplant's fortune:** it is a prediction about this plant's own future — the cat, the sun, the new leaf — not a slogan or a care instruction.
- **Written for this plant in this window:** the windowsill, the sun's warmest spot, the brave new leaf — it could not be said of any plant in any room.
- **Funny in a way a plant lover laughs at:** the cat surrendering the windowsill is the keeper's daily battle, and the plant winning it is the joke that lands.
- **Kind and warm:** the sun saves its warmest spot, a brave new leaf appears — no doom, no withering, no mockery.
- **Fortune cadence:** "This week ..." opens like a prediction and reads cleanly aloud, three beats building to the new leaf.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully
deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no input, no randomness, no
network. A gate inside the program refuses to write anything outside
1–120 characters or more than one line.
